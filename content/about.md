title: "About this place"
---

Hello, my name is Ben. You have stumbled onto my private little corner of the Internet, or at least, one of them. This is an intentionally simple, lightweight space where I will share a single photo every day until I either run out of photos or get bored of this whole thing. I have been taking photos since around 2011, however I have never taken the time to actually process many of them or share them anywhere. My goal is not necessarily to share *all* of them, but to make an interesting gallery over time which coalesces into something unique.

Anyway, however you found this place, I hope you enjoy your time here. Bookmark it and spend 20 seconds here a day, and then move on with your life. You know, if you want to.
