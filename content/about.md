---
title: "About this place"
---

Hello, my name is Ben. You have stumbled onto my private little corner of the Internet, or at least, one of them. This is an intentionally simple, lightweight space where I will share a single photo every day until I either run out of photos or get bored of this whole thing.

I have been taking photos since around 2011, however I have never taken the time to actually process many of them or share them anywhere. My goal is not necessarily to share *all* of them, but to make an interesting gallery over time which coalesces into something unique. Along the way, you may also find small notes attached to these photos, describing the circumstances in which they were taken, small memories attached to them, etc. You may also find nothing of substance whatsoever.

Anyway, however you came to this place, I hope you enjoy your time here. Bookmark it and spend 20-60 seconds here sometime during your day, and then move on with your life. You know, if you want to.
